Business Insights & Recommendations
Derived from SQL analysis · directly actionable
📈
Q4 revenue opportunity: Oct–Dec drives 38% of annual revenue. Prepare inventory & campaigns 6 weeks early to maximize peak-season capture.
👥
Loyalty gap: Only 12% of customers are Loyal (5+ orders). Converting Returning to Loyal adds ~$2,130 avg CLV per customer.
⚠️
Revenue at risk: 389 at-risk customers hold $184k in historical spend. A targeted win-back campaign is the highest-ROI retention action.
🌍
South region underperforms by ~15% in customer count despite similar market size. Region-specific campaigns could close the gap within 2 quarters.
